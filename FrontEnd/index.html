3 · AI Proxy LambdaReads the HF token from SSM Parameter Store and calls the AI models
4 · History LambdaPython 3.12 on arm64 in private subnets across 2 AZs
5 · DynamoDBReached privately through a Gateway VPC Endpoint with no NAT cost
